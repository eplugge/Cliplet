## Description
Cliplet quietly remembers everything you copy — text, links, images, and files — and keeps
it a click away in your menu bar.

Open it, type to filter your history, and click any clip to put it back on the clipboard.
Preview anything with Quick Look, pin the clips you reuse, and keep sensitive apps out with
per-app exclusions.

• Lightweight menu-bar app — no Dock clutter, native and fast
• Searchable history — type to filter, click or Return to copy a clip back
• Quick Look previews — press Space to preview any clip, text included
• Pin & promote — pin the clips you reuse; recent ones bubble to the top
• ⌘1–9 quick copy — grab your top clips without reaching for the mouse
• Per-app exclusions — never capture from the apps you choose
• Tunable — history size, visible rows, and maximum clip size
• Light & dark — follows your system appearance

Everything stays on your Mac. Cliplet has no account, no network, and collects no data.
